# Insight AI Product Vision

Generated: 2026-06-08

## North Star

Insight AI becomes the GEO operating system for brands and agencies.

It should continuously answer:

- Why are we not recommended?
- Who is winning instead?
- Which sources does AI trust?
- Which pages or topics are missing?
- What should we do this week?
- Did last month’s work improve visibility?

## Product Promise

> Turn AI search visibility into prioritized GEO actions.

## Product Pillars

### 1. Diagnose

Website/page audits, SRO readiness, AI crawler access, schema, FAQ, content depth, rendering risk, and citation readiness.

### 2. Discover

Prompt opportunities, persona fan-out, niche query discovery, citation opportunities, competitor gaps, and market topics.

### 3. Explain

Battlecards, evidence, confidence, sources, and “why this score is not higher.”

### 4. Act

30/60/90-day plans, tasks, expected GEO gain, difficulty, owner/status, and agency-ready reports.

### 5. Learn

Memory, change detection, recommendation effectiveness, and trend analysis.

## Differentiation

Insight AI should not compete by claiming the largest prompt database first. It should compete by being the most actionable product for a paying customer:

- Regional Arabic/English support.
- Agency white-label workflow.
- Evidence-first trust layer.
- Website audit plus AI visibility in one flow.
- Citation and competitor gaps converted into tasks.

## Final Direction

Insight AI should feel less like analytics software and more like a GEO strategist that already knows the customer’s next move.
