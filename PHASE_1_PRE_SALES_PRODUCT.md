# Phase 1: Pre-Sales Product

Goal: make customers willing to pay before launch.

## Core Experience

The customer enters a website and sees:

- why AI search is not recommending them;
- who is winning instead;
- which citations they are missing;
- which page/content gaps matter;
- what to do in the next 30 days.

## Build

- SRO-enhanced GEO Audit.
- Why You're Not Recommended page as primary sales surface.
- Competitor Battlecard report.
- Citation Opportunity Finder.
- 30-day GEO Action Plan.
- Executive Audit PDF.
- Agency white-label audit export.

## Current Progress

- Existing GEO audit has been upgraded with tracker-derived AEO/SRO checks:
  - AI bot access.
  - `llms-full.txt`.
  - server-rendered content.
  - CSR risk.
  - noscript fallback.
  - JavaScript weight.
  - page size.
  - language tag.

## Success Criteria

- Prospect receives useful audit results in under two minutes.
- Every recommendation has evidence and expected impact.
- Report is strong enough for an agency to show a client.
