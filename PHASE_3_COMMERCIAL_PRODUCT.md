# Phase 3: Commercial Product

Goal: convert pre-sales audit value into SaaS revenue.

## Build

- Scheduled monitoring with BullMQ/Redis.
- Task owner, status, due dates, and effectiveness tracking.
- Agency operations center.
- White-label report templates.
- Subscription limits and feature gating.
- Report automation.
- Citation/source authority scoring.
- Competitor page comparison at scale.

## Success Criteria

- Agencies can sell GEO retainers powered by Insight AI.
- Customers receive weekly value without manual analysis.
- Reports connect actions to visibility, citations, and GEO score movement.
